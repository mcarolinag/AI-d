ℹ️ How to Use the Tool

  • 1. Submit a Project Proposal: Enter the details of your project.
    - Write down a detailed project description like the example below!
  • 2. View Similar Results: The AI agent will find the Top 5 - 15 most similar projects.
  • 3. Explore the Map: Use the interactive map to view where the similar projects were implemented.
  • 4. Use the Results for Decision-Making: Gain strategic insights from the results. You may determine whether the submitted project is potentially redundant or complementary.

📝 Example of Project Description

This project's primary objective is to establish a cancer-care specialized medical center along with its professionally trained personnel in Soran Province, Erbil Governorate, Kurdistan Regional Government of Iraq. Presently, KRG doesn't have a cancer-care specialized healthcare center and also the doctors lack capacity in this area. This project will establish a healthcare center which would focus on cancer diagnosis, medical treatment and also surgery. Cancer care specialized doctors and nurses would be trained in Korea and also professional equipment would be provided for cancer detection and treatment. Once the center is established, it would serve as the main cancer center in all Kurdistan, serving approximately 10 million people.
